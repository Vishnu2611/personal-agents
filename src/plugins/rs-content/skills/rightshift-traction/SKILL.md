# RightShift Traction

A reference guide, "Traction Validation and Early Customer Acquisition", for startup founders and business
owners preparing to launch ad campaigns with Google Ads, Meta Ads and AdRoll. It is ordered as five steps:
ad platforms, analytics platforms, AdRoll, campaign launches, then ad copy and images, plus a resource list.

## When to use

When a founder or client asks how to get ad platforms, tracking and first campaigns live, or needs one step
of that chain explained or checked. Note it is an instructional guide rather than a generate-an-artifact
prompt: use it to walk the user through the steps, or answer questions from it, one step at a time. The user
should say which step (or what stage) they are at.

## Not for

Client deliverables. It does not research a market, write LinkedIn posts or case studies (see the other
rs-content skills), or run campaigns for the user; it explains the setup.

## How to run

Read `resources/prompt.md` and follow it exactly — it is the canonical guide (source:
`src/plugins/rs-content/prompts/rightshift-traction/current/rightshift-traction-v1.md`). Ask the user where
they are in the five steps and which accounts already exist, then guide them through the relevant steps
in order. Ignore the literal "[Table of Contents]" placeholder near the top.
