## 3. Training client — MeridianRx (Days 1–5)

**Given to the lead on Day 1:**

> MeridianRx is a regional pharmacy chain, 60 stores across Georgia, Alabama and South Carolina. Dana Whitfield, VP of Marketing, has engaged us to build "a loyalty app — points, tiers, badges, the works. Our customers should feel rewarded for choosing us. CVS and Walgreens both have one and we're losing ground."
>
> Budget signal: meaningful. Timeline signal: "before the holidays."

### Evidence pack, released progressively

**Day 1 release:**
- Store-level revenue: maintenance-medication refills are 61% of pharmacy revenue
- Dana's one-page brief, which mentions points 9 times and customers 0 times

**Day 2 release, unlocked only by good questions:**
- Pharmacy system data: 34% of refill reminders result in no pickup within 7 days
- 40 support tickets. Clusters: "didn't know it was ready" (14), "cost was different than expected" (9), "needed doctor approval and nobody told me" (8), "couldn't get there before close" (6), other (3)
- Two pharmacist interview notes. Patients abandon at the counter when the price is higher than last time
- Churned-customer verbatim: "I found out at the window it needed a new prescription. I'd driven 20 minutes."
- Competitor scan: the loyalty program Dana admires is a front-of-store retail program, not a pharmacy one

Nothing in the pack shows customers asking for points. That absence is deliberate.

### Claude as Dana Whitfield

Dana is cooperative and confident. The evidence does not support her belief.

- Her first answer to any "why" is directional: *"because our customers want to feel valued."*
- The second and third probe produce the real sources — a conference talk, a board question about engagement, one franchise owner who complained.
- A question aimed at a number or a source releases the matching evidence.
- A vague question gets a vague answer and the evidence stays locked.
- She pushes back if the lead proposes dropping points outright. She needs a bridge.

**The gap to find:** MeridianRx's outcome is refill retention. Points address none of the four abandonment causes. Three of the four can be solved by delivering information at the right moment.
