## Step 2 -Building a brand guideline for your startup

### Description

Branding is how your users will perceive you. It's a combination of meaning, experience, visuals, and associations. Even at the early Testable Product stage, you can start shaping your brand—one that will evolve alongside your product.

For a brand guideline at this stage, it's essential to focus on three key elements:

1. Colors
2. Typography
3. Font

These foundational choices will help establish a strong and recognizable identity for your startup from day one.

## Step 4 - Creating User Flows

As a startup founder, you have a vision for your product—but turning that vision into a fully functional product requires careful planning. That's where **prototypes** come in.

A prototype is a visual representation of your product that helps you to be ensure that Nothing Is Overlooked – by mapping out key pages and features, you can identify gaps before development begins.

Creating user flows can help you build a product that truly works for your users. Start prototyping early to **reduce risks and set your startup up for success!**

### Description

A user flow is a visual representation of the path a user takes through your product to accomplish a specific goal. Creating a clear user flow for your Testable Product is essential for several reasons:

- **Clarify product functionality**: They help you define exactly what features your Testable Product needs to include
- **Identify potential issues**: They reveal pain points and friction in the user experience early
- **Streamline development**: They provide clear guidance for developers on what to build
- **Focus on core value**: They help you prioritize features that deliver your main value proposition
- **Communicate with stakeholders**: They make it easier to explain your product vision

### Tools

Claude
Mermaid Live

### Step-by-Step Process to Create a User Flow With Claude

1. **Define your target user.** Tell about your primary user personas. Share their goals, pain points, and motivations
2. **Identify key user objectives.** What main tasks will users want to accomplish? Which problem are you solving for them?
3. **Map the entry points.** How will users discover and enter your product? What's their starting point? (app store, website, email link, etc.)
4. **Outline the core user journey.** Describe the steps from entry to goal completion. Include all screens/interactions needed
5. **Ask to review and optimize.** Claude will evaluate the flow for unnecessary steps, Identify potential confusion points and Simplify wherever possible
6. **Visualize the flow.** Claude can help create a diagram showing the complete journey. It can be in different formats (flowchart, wireframes, etc.)
7. **Test assumptions.** Discuss how to validate your user flow with real users. Plan for feedback collection and iteration

> 💡 **Advice**
> When working with Claude, provide as much context as possible about your product idea, target users, and business goals. Claude can then help you craft a user flow that balances simplicity (essential for an early Testable Product) with delivering real value to your users.

> 💡 **Advice**
> For high-quality image exports, copy the generated Claude code and use [Mermaid Live](https://mermaid.live/). In the **Actions** tab, select **Export as PNG**, and adjust the width and height settings (we recommend using **5000** for the best results)

## Step 5 - Creating Wireframes

### Testable Product Wireframes

With a product wireframe, you can confidently move forward, knowing your advertising website will attract and convert customers effectively. It's a smart first step toward online success! Clarify product functionality: They help you define exactly what features your Testable Product needs to include. This visual representation of your product helps you to ensure that Nothing Is Overlooked – by mapping out key pages and features, you can identify gaps before development begins.

Even a simple **wireframe** can help you build a product that truly works for your users. Start prototyping early to **reduce risks and set your startup up for success!**

### Description

A wireframe is a simplified, visual blueprint of a digital product's layout, focusing on structure and functionality rather than detailed design. Creating clear wireframes for your Testable Product is essential for several reasons:

- Act as a **visual guide** for the layout and structure of a digital product.
- Highlight the placement and **hierarchy of key elements** on a page.
- **Facilitate collaboration** and communication between team members, designers, and stakeholders.
- Identify potential **usability issues** early in the design process.
- Serve as a **foundation for testing and refining** ideas early.
- Ensure alignment on **design objectives** and **functionality** across the project team

> - Include notes for any interactive elements or animations
